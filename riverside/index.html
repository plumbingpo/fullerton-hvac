Keeping Your Riverside HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.

Comfort Challenges Riverside Homeowners Face

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
